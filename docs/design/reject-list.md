# Design Reject List

Hard rules for every CountryStateCity design: the website, the product apps, docs themes, social images and anything else that carries the brand. Anything listed here must never be used. Check new directions and design-system tokens against this list before sharing them.

**Owner:** Darshan Gada · **Last updated:** 27 September 2026

## Backgrounds

| Rejected | Examples | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Cream, beige, ivory, sand or any paper-toned off-white background | `#F4F0E6`, `#EAE3D3`, `#FAF9F5` | Too close to the Claude look. Not wanted for CSC. |
| Dark or dark-first designs | Navy or black page backgrounds, dark hero sections | Darshan does not like dark designs. Light-first only. |

## Colours

| Rejected | Examples | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Orange, terracotta or clay as a brand, accent or button colour | `#C2410C`, `#D97757`, `#F97316` | Too close to the Claude look. Not wanted for CSC. |

## Fonts
